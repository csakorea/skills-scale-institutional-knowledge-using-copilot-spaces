# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Communication Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Communication Leads manage engagement with internal and external stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure expectations are set clearly, feedback is collected systematically, and communication remains transparent and timely.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and maintain stakeholder engagement plans
- Collect, synthesize, and relay stakeholder feedback to the project team
- Set and manage stakeholder expectations throughout project phases
- Coordinate stakeholder communications and status updates
- Identify and escalate stakeholder concerns or misalignments
- Facilitate stakeholder reviews and approval gates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Reports to**: Project Manager
- **Collaborates with**: Product Manager (on requirement clarification and feature prioritization), Project Manager (on timeline and risk communication), Developers (on progress updates and technical feasibility questions)
- **Receives input from**: All team members on progress and blockers
- **Provides updates to**: Stakeholders, Executive Sponsors, Governance bodies

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and satisfaction
- Reduce miscommunication and rework due to unclear expectations
- Enable timely escalation and resolution of stakeholder concerns
- Facilitate productive collaboration between stakeholders and the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and town halls
- Status reports and progress dashboards
- Feedback synthesis documents
- Issue escalation notices

### Required Skills & Experience
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to translate between technical and business language
- Experience managing diverse stakeholder groups
- Excellent documentation and synthesis abilities

---

## Risk & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Officers identify, monitor, and manage project risks while ensuring compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements. They provide strategic guidance on risk mitigation and maintain the risk register throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate risk identification workshops and assessments
- Maintain and update the risk register with impact, probability, and mitigation strategies
- Escalate critical risks to leadership with recommended actions
- Monitor compliance with organizational standards and regulatory requirements
- Advise on risk response strategies (avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept)
- Conduct post-project risk retrospectives to capture lessons learned
- Provide risk awareness training to project teams

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with**: Project Manager (on risk management and escalation), Developers (on technical risks), Product Manager (on business and scope risks), Stakeholders (on compliance and strategic risks)
- **Reports to**: Project Manager and Project Sponsor
- **Advises**: Leadership and governance committees

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions from unforeseen risks
- Ensure regulatory and organizational compliance
- Enable informed decision-making through clear risk analysis
- Build organizational risk awareness and mitigation capability

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and heat maps
- Risk mitigation plans and status updates
- Compliance checklists and audit reports
- Executive risk briefings
- Escalation notifications

### Required Skills & Experience
- Deep understanding of organizational policies and regulatory landscape
- Risk assessment and analysis expertise
- Project management experience
- Strong leadership and communication skills

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and maintain quality standards for project deliverables. They oversee testing strategies, validate that acceptance criteria are met, and work closely with developers and project managers to ensure consistent delivery of high-quality outputs.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria validation processes
- Develop and execute comprehensive testing strategies (unit, integration, system, UAT)
- Oversee test planning, execution, and defect tracking
- Validate that all acceptance criteria are met before release
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and quality improvements
- Report quality metrics and trends to the project manager
- Facilitate quality reviews and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Partners with**: Development Team (on test planning and issue resolution), Project Manager (on quality issues and timeline impact), Product Manager (on acceptance criteria interpretation), Stakeholder Communication Lead (on quality communications)
- **Reports to**: Project Manager
- **Escalates to**: Project Manager and Product Manager (on critical quality issues)

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet defined quality standards
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, reliable testing and deployment cycles
- Provide clear visibility into quality status

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Quality status reports in project meetings
- Acceptance validation sign-offs

### Required Skills & Experience
- Strong testing methodology knowledge (manual and automated)
- Quality assurance tools and practices expertise
- Attention to detail and systematic thinking
- Collaboration and communication skills

---

## Technical Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architecture Leads ensure that technical design decisions align with organizational standards, scalability requirements, and best practices. They coach the development team on architecture principles and provide guidance on complex technical trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design proposals
- Establish and maintain technical standards and guidelines
- Coach the development team on architecture best practices
- Advise on scalability, performance, and security considerations
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure integration with existing systems and platforms
- Facilitate technical design reviews and knowledge sharing

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Supports**: Development Team (on technical guidance and design reviews)
- **Advises**: Product Manager (on technical feasibility and constraints), Project Manager (on technical risks and timeline impact)
- **Collaborates with**: Quality Assurance Lead (on testability and performance standards)
- **Escalates to**: Project Manager and organizational technical leadership (on architectural issues)

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and consistency across projects
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable scalable, maintainable solutions
- Build technical capability across the organization

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Design review sessions and technical guidance documents
- Technical feasibility assessments
- Best practice guidelines and standards documentation

### Required Skills & Experience
- Deep technical expertise in relevant domains
- System design and architecture experience
- Understanding of organizational technology strategy
- Strong mentoring and communication skills

---

## Project Budget & Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Project Budget & Resource Managers handle financial planning and resource allocation for projects. They track budget expenditures, manage resource allocation across competing priorities, and escalate budget risks to leadership.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain project budgets aligned with scope and timeline
- Track actual spending against budget and identify variances
- Allocate and schedule resources (team members, contractors, infrastructure)
- Manage resource conflicts and optimize utilization
- Escalate budget overruns and resource constraints
- Forecast financial impact of scope changes and risks
- Provide financial status reporting to leadership and stakeholders
- Optimize resource costs without compromising quality

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with**: Project Manager (on resource planning and budget tracking), Product Manager (on scope and priority changes affecting budget), Developers (on resource availability and estimation)
- **Reports to**: Project Manager, Project Sponsor, Finance leadership
- **Coordinates with**: HR and other projects on resource allocation

### Goals
- Deliver projects within approved budgets
- Optimize resource utilization across the organization
- Provide transparent financial visibility
- Enable informed decision-making on project trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Budget plans and financial forecasts
- Resource allocation schedules
- Budget variance reports and analysis
- Financial impact assessments for scope changes
- Executive financial briefings

### Required Skills & Experience
- Project financial management and budgeting expertise
- Resource planning and scheduling experience
- Strong analytical and forecasting skills
- Stakeholder communication and negotiation abilities
